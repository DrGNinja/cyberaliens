Cyb3rALIENS

A connected device can be seen and still not be allowed to speak. Cyb3rALIENS puts two chips in the device. They make one key. The device gets an identity. A record is written. Only then can it talk, privately, to another device with the same record. A device with no record stays visible and is refused.

You do not need the patent to watch it. Press play. The screen fills from the chips up to the channel.

Watch the system Register a device, then another. Type a message and send it. An unregistered device is refused.
